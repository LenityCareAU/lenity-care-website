Our Services

Comprehensive disability support tailored to your needs

Support Coordination

Who provides it: Qualified support coordinators with NDIS experience.

How it helps: We guide you through your NDIS plan, help you access services, and ensure you get the support you need.

Process: Initial assessment → Planning → Ongoing coordination & advocacy

Psychosocial Recovery Coaching

Focus: Mental health recovery with a strength-based, person-centered approach.

Specific Outcomes: Increased independence, resilience, social connection, and hope.

Integration: Fully aligned with your NDIS plan goals and recovery journey.

Early Childhood Intervention

Age Range: Birth to school age children with developmental needs.

Support Areas: Speech, physical development, cognitive, social, and emotional support.

Approach: Family-centered, play-based intervention in natural environments.

Home Modifications

What we offer: Safe, accessible home modifications for independence and safety.

Process: Assessment → Design → Implementation → Follow-up

Examples: Ramps, handrails, bathroom modifications, mobility aids installation.

Personal Training

Focus: Accessible fitness and health support tailored to your abilities.

Qualifications: Certified, disability-aware fitness professionals.

Outcomes: Improved health, strength, confidence, and community engagement.

How to Access Our Services

For NDIS Participants

You can access our services through your NDIS plan. Simply contact us to discuss your needs, and we'll help you navigate the process.

Funding & Costs

Most of our services are funded through the NDIS. We work with participants to maximize their plan funds and ensure they get the best value support.

About Lenity Care Australia

Committed to person-centered, culturally safe disability support

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Lenity Care Australia was created with a simple but powerful mission: to provide culturally safe, person-centered disability support services that respect the diversity and individual needs of every participant.

We believe that every person deserves high-quality support that honors their culture, language, and aspirations. That's why we've built a team of passionate, qualified professionals dedicated to making a real difference in the lives of NDIS participants across Greater Sydney.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NDIS?

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) is an Australian government program that provides funding and support for eligible people with disabilities.

How do I access your services?

If you're an NDIS participant, you can request our services through your NDIS plan. Contact us to discuss your needs and how we can help.

What qualifications do your staff have?

Our team includes qualified support coordinators, recovery coaches, early intervention specialists, and personal trainers with relevant experience and certifications.

What if I'm not happy with a service?

Your feedback is important to us. We have a clear complaints procedure to address any concerns. Please contact us directly.
